Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Inquiries

What are the primary eligibility criteria?

Applicants must have a minimum clear high school/secondary completion (10+2 standard layout). Selection cycles weigh creative orientation, critical reasoning aptitude, and personal performance profiles during custom interview stages.

Do you provide real industrial internship support?

Yes. Our structural production partnerships loop students into leading Indian broadcasting houses, OTT networks, and production cells during the final semesters of execution.

Are weekend alternative tracks available for working professionals?

Our comprehensive Screenwriting modules, Digital Photography classes, and specialized Film Direction bootcamps include dedicated weekend schedules to handle alternative timing requirements comfortably.
